About

I have always been drawn to images, objects, places, and the details that can easily go unnoticed. Looking closely often changes the way something feels, and sometimes becomes the beginning of a new direction.

My work moves between drawing, photography, painting, pattern, and other forms of making. I like the freedom of moving between them rather than being defined by one particular medium.

A photograph, a line, a colour, a surface, or something found can all become a starting point. From there, I tend to work intuitively, allowing things to change through repetition, editing, combination, and simply trying something different.

Sometimes an idea arrives clearly; other times it reveals itself through the work. I follow those changes rather than trying to decide too early what something should become, allowing the image, mark, colour, or material to take its own direction.
