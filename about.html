About

Years of looking closely at products have shaped a way of seeing that goes beyond function — into proportion, detail, material, finish, and the experience of the whole.

A background rooted in product, together with a strong sense of vision and aesthetic, informs a particular approach to creating products — physical or digital — considered in every detail, with the aim of making things that feel relevant, desirable, and worth living with.

Creating products you would want to own is ultimately about more than the object itself. It is about the quality of the service, the way it is presented, received, handled, and remembered — considering the entire journey, from the first encounter to the destination.

Immersion in the subject is essential: understanding the context, the materials, the details, and the intentions that give a product its character.

For me, the product is the extension.

The experience is the journey.
