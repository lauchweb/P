Arbre Nuage

A place for things made slowly, considered carefully, and lived with for a long time.

Sometimes an address is not something you search for.

Someone gives it to you.

They write it down, tell you a little about the road, and say you should go.

This is that kind of place.

For people who notice

For people who have travelled without needing much.

Who have slept somewhere unfamiliar, crossed a country by road, spent an afternoon in a field, or learned something useful from someone they had just met.

People who recognise a good thing before somebody has explained why it is good.

Curious about materials. Curious about places. Curious about people. Comfortable with the simple things.

By appointment

You come in.

We talk for a while.

There are things to look at and things to touch.

You may leave with nothing.

That is perfectly fine.

The beginning is usually somewhere between a conversation and a piece of material.
Materials are meant to be handled.
No
hurry.
Some ideas need a few days.

Some materials need another attempt.

Some decisions are better made after you have gone home.

We let them.

Eventually, something leaves the workshop.

It goes with you.

Then it starts becoming yours.

Wear it. Travel with it. Mark it. Repair it. Change it when life changes.

We would like our things to become better records of a life, rather than things kept away from it.

We like being around things that already know how to exist.
Trees. Plants. Weather. Old houses. Roads. Skin. Light. Things that do not need to announce themselves.

Arbre Nuage

The tree, the cloud, the rain that passes between them.

Some things are so fundamental that we can almost forget how much they give us.
Somewhere in the middle of this strange world, a place to slow down.
