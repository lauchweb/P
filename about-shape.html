An address you may hear about from someone you meet somewhere.

Someone tells you about a place.

Maybe they write the street down for you on a piece of paper.

They tell you that you should go.

Arbre Nuage

Not everyone needs to know where it is.

Some people are simply curious enough to find out.

People who travel. People who notice things. People who have spent time somewhere without needing to turn it into a story.

People who understand when something is worth staying with.

You come by appointment.

We talk.

You look around.

You touch things.

Materials. Cloth. Leather. Objects.

You might leave without anything.

Things are better understood in the hand.
Take
your time.
There is no rush here.

You return another day.

We talk again.

Something begins to take shape.
We like the world around the things we make.
Trees. Plants. Old houses. Roads. Weather. Places where people have lived for a long time.
We are interested in things that feel natural because they are natural.

A place somewhere in the middle of this strange world.

A breath.

Perhaps one day someone will give you our address.
