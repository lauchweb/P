About

We believe some things are better when they are given time.

Arbre Nuage began with a simple feeling: that there are things in life we should experience properly.

Not quickly. Not because they are available. Not because someone told us we need them.

We wanted a place where an object could take the time it needs to become itself.

And where a person could take the time to become part of that process too.

The name

Arbre Nuage means tree, cloud.



The tree and the cloud are connected in ways that are very simple and very deep.



Things that give. Things that receive. Things that make life possible.



There are things we believe we should not leave life without experiencing.

We don't make things quickly.

You come by appointment.

We talk.

You touch the materials.

You tell us things about yourself. Sometimes directly. Sometimes without saying much.

Then you leave.

Maybe we meet again another day.

The piece may take months.

That is not a problem. That is part of it.

We are a passionate team, but not quite the usual one.



We spend a lot of time thinking about materials, construction, development, function and good gear.



Sometimes we develop a material ourselves. Sometimes we look for one somewhere else. Either way, we are very selective.

We don't think the relationship with an object ends when you take it home.

A good piece should endure time.

And if something happens to it, you should be able to come back.

We can alter it. We can repair it. We can keep working on it.

It is your piece.

Let it become yours.

We like patina.

But not the kind that is made to look old before it has lived.

We mean the marks that arrive because you used something.

Because you travelled with it. Because you worked in it. Because it went somewhere with you.

Time should leave a trace.

We are fascinated by things that feel completely natural.

Good proportion. Natural light. The relief of skin. Soft skin. A face that does not need to perform for the camera.

We like the things around the things we make.

A large piece of land. Endemic plants. An old house. A workshop. An office. A place where the work and the world around it can exist together.

We are drawn to people who have travelled, whether they had much money for it or not.

People who went by backpack, by car, by whatever was possible.

People who have endured some things. People from different places. People who speak several languages.

People who understand quickly when something is worth knowing.

People who understand why trees matter.

People who like touching materials, trying things, experimenting.

Adventurous people who can still be extremely simple in everyday life.

That is closer to our DNA.

We believe good things take time to do.

And that a good thing should have enough time to become part of a life.

Arbre Nuage is a place for that.

For making. For looking. For touching. For talking. For waiting. For finding something worth keeping.

And for coming back.
